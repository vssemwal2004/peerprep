# PeerPrep Public Landing Page — Complete Content Brief

**Document purpose:** Give the landing-page designer a complete, accurate view of what PeerPrep offers and what content should appear on the public website.

**Audience for this document:** Product designer, UI designer, copywriter, and marketing reviewer.

**Page audience:** Students, college placement teams, faculty/coordinators, university decision-makers, and visitors who do not yet know PeerPrep.

**Important scope:** This is a public-facing content brief. It intentionally excludes software architecture, APIs, database details, deployment, internal implementation, and admin-screen specifications that do not help a visitor understand the product.

---

## 1. What the landing page must communicate

PeerPrep is an end-to-end placement preparation platform that brings together:

- Structured learning paths
- Coding and DSA practice
- Curated QuickPrep question sets
- Timed and proctored assessments
- Live peer mock interviews
- AI voice interviews with ANNU
- Performance and placement-readiness analytics
- Adaptive challenges, XP, levels, and badges
- University and global rankings based on Prep Score
- Resume building and PDF export
- University tools for managing students, content, tests, interviews, and outcomes

The page should make one idea clear:

> Students should not have to prepare across disconnected tools. PeerPrep connects learning, practice, assessment, interview preparation, and measurable improvement in one placement journey.

### Recommended primary positioning

**Headline:** Learn. Practice. Prove you are ready.

**Supporting line:** PeerPrep brings guided learning, coding practice, secure assessments, mock interviews, and performance insights into one placement preparation platform.

**Primary student CTA:** Student login

**Primary institution CTA:** Contact us

**Optional discovery CTA:** Explore the platform

Do not use **“Get started for free”** unless public self-registration and a free plan are actually available. The current product is primarily accessed through an institution-provided student account.

---

## 2. Core story of the platform

The landing page should present PeerPrep as a connected preparation cycle:

1. **Learn** through semester-wise subjects, videos, notes, and question resources.
2. **Practice** with topic-wise, company-wise, and curated coding problems.
3. **Assess** readiness through timed tests and real coding evaluations.
4. **Interview** with peers and the ANNU AI voice interviewer.
5. **Improve** using analytics, feedback, Prep Score, goals, and benchmarks.

This connected loop is more important than presenting PeerPrep as only a coding website or only an assessment platform.

## 11. Proof and trust content

The page will be stronger if it includes evidence, but fabricated numbers must never be used.

### Approved types of proof

- Number of active students — only from verified product data
- Number of connected institutions — only from verified product data
- Number of coding questions — only from a current catalog count
- Number of assessments completed — only from verified data
- Number of mock interviews completed — only from verified data
- Verified student or faculty testimonials with name/role/institution permission
- Approved institution logos
- Product screenshots using safe demo data

### Placeholder format for the designer

- `[Verified student count]+ students preparing`
- `[Verified problem count]+ coding questions`
- `[Verified assessment count]+ assessment attempts`
- `[Verified institution count] institutions connected`

## 13. FAQ copy bank

The public page should show approximately 8–12 of these questions, with the rest available on demand if needed.

### What is PeerPrep?

PeerPrep is an end-to-end placement preparation platform for guided learning, coding practice, assessments, mock interviews, performance analytics, challenges, and career preparation.

### Who is PeerPrep for?

PeerPrep is built for college students preparing for internships and placements, and for the faculty, coordinators, and placement teams who guide them.

### How do students get access?

Students usually receive access through their institution. Available modules can vary according to the institution’s PeerPrep setup.

### Can I practise DSA and coding questions?

Yes. PrepCoding supports topic-wise, company-wise, and difficulty-based problem discovery, along with a coding workspace, test cases, submissions, and editorials where available.

### What is QuickPrep?

QuickPrep is a focused set of coding questions created around a company, topic, or preparation goal. Students can track progress and earn configured rewards after completing a set.

### What kinds of assessments are supported?

Assessments can include MCQ, one-line, short-answer, coding, or mixed sections. They can also include schedules, timers, secure setup, monitoring controls, and result reports.

### Does PeerPrep include proctoring?

Institutions can enable browser, camera, audio-event, and AI-assisted monitoring controls for assessments. These controls support review and integrity policies, but no browser-based system can prevent every possible violation.

### How do peer mock interviews work?

Students are paired as interviewer and interviewee, agree on a session time, complete the mock interview, and submit structured feedback that contributes to interview analytics.

### What is ANNU?

ANNU is PeerPrep’s AI voice interview experience. It presents structured questions, accepts spoken answers, creates a transcript for review, and can ask configured follow-up questions.

### Can ANNU use my resume?

An AI interview can use the student’s saved resume as question context only when that interview requires it and the student explicitly consents.

### What is Prep Score?

Prep Score is a verified progress signal shown inside PeerPrep. It helps students understand their own preparation and ranking context without exposing the internal scoring formula.

### Are rankings public?

No public student directory is used for global ranking. A signed-in student sees only their own university and global position. Global comparison uses aggregate score distributions rather than sharing names or contact details.

### What can I see in analytics?

Students can see readiness, consistency, coding accuracy, topic coverage, assessment trends, learning completion, interview feedback, and company benchmark gaps when enough evidence is available.

### Does a readiness score guarantee placement?

No. Readiness and company benchmarks are preparation guidance based on available evidence, not a prediction or guarantee of selection.

### Can I build a resume in PeerPrep?

Yes. The resume builder supports flexible sections, live preview, quality guidance, reordering, visibility controls, and professional PDF download.

### What are Prep Coins?

Prep Coins are institution-issued credits that can unlock selected priced features where enabled. They are separate from Prep Score and never improve a student’s rank.

### Can institutions control which modules are available?

Yes. Institutions can configure access to learning, coding, assessments, interviews, progression, and other modules according to their program.

### Can faculty create content and assessments?

Authorized faculty and coordinators can manage learning content, reusable questions, coding problems, QuickPrep sets, assessments, interview events, and student feedback according to their permissions.

### Is student progress tracked across modules?

Yes. PeerPrep combines verified activity from enabled modules to show progress, consistency, readiness, challenges, and recommended actions.

### Does every student see every feature?

Not necessarily. Module availability, content, assessment rules, interview programs, and Prep Coin pricing can vary by institution and account access.
